## Contributing

**Name your PRs like this:**
- `Add: <project/demo name>` — new entries
- `Fix: <what was fixed>` — corrections
- `Remove: <what and why>` — deletions

**Every entry needs:**
1. A link to the original source (repo, tweet, or article) — no secondary reposts
2. One line on what GPT-6 Astra capability it demonstrates
3. Real traction numbers if available (likes/views/stars), copied from the source — never estimated

**Quality bar:** directly about GPT-6 Astra (not general GPT content). Demos must be reproducible or verifiable via the author's own post.

Thank you for keeping this list verifiable! 🌟
